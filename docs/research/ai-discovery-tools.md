# AI discovery and extraction on a nonprofit budget

Research checked **2026-11-11 (UTC)** for branch `opinionated`. Scope: AI-assisted discovery, HTML fetching, extraction, verification and volunteer maintenance. Deterministic city datasets, OSM/Overpass and calendar/feed adapters are the parallel researcher's scope; they are seeds here, not re-researched transports.

Read first: `docs/spec.md`, `docs/sources.md`, `docs/library-sources.md`, `docs/effect-architecture.md`. This is a proposal, not an implemented ingestion system or verified inventory of classes. No accounts, credentials, purchases, installations, model calls or source ingestion were used. Research used public documentation retrieval/search; unavailable/protected pages were not bypassed.

**Evidence labels:** **VERIFIED** means an official page was checked on **2026-11-11**, at the linked URL; it does not establish account eligibility, a working API integration or scraper permission. **UNVERIFIED** means the page did not establish the stated detail, was inaccessible, or the idea needs follow-up. **ESTIMATE / PROPOSED** identifies our arithmetic, workload assumptions and proposed operational limits, not vendor promises. All dollar amounts are USD, before tax. Prices are API prices, not consumer chat subscriptions. Signup credits and annual prepayments are explicitly distinguished from recurring allowances.

## Recommendation

Start with volunteer-approved organization URLs and **one** recurring free search API, ordinary conditional GETs, local HTML cleanup, and a single structured extraction call only when relevant content changes. Prefer **`claude-haiku-5-5`** for the small paid, predictable option. Use a second inexpensive provider only for disputed classifications. Verify evidence in code before the existing atomic Supabase ingestion RPC. Keep blocked pages and uncertain claims out of public results; an honest empty result is preferable to a hallucinated free class.

An entirely free variant uses Gemini's unpaid tier for public text, subject to its data-use terms and account quotas, or Workers AI with a daily inference budget. Neither is a guarantee of enough free capacity. At the largest requested volume, the recommended paid extraction path still estimates **$10.65/month** if every page needs extraction, including a small judge allowance and contingency, conditional on free runner/hosting eligibility. Batch lowers that to about **$5.58**. Human maintenance and permitted access matter more than token cost.

**Deployment dependency:** Vercel Hobby is restricted to non-commercial, personal use; nonprofit status alone does not establish eligibility. **VERIFIED:** [Hobby terms/limits](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby). Obtain a terms determination or suitable hosting arrangement before treating this organization's production hosting as free. All totals below assume an eligible existing hosting arrangement; they do not silently add a paid Vercel plan.

## 1. Discovery: search for organizations, then verify their own pages

Use a small rotating query matrix: discipline (`martial arts`, `karate`, `judo`, `boxing`, `tai chi`, `self defense`, `open mat`) × borough/neighborhood × explicit cost wording. Include youth/adult and nonprofit/community-center variants. Example leads-only query: `"free" "self defense" Harlem registration`. Deduplicate URLs/domains, strip tracking parameters conservatively, retain discovery query/date, and follow the organizer's canonical page. Search snippets, archived text and LLM answers never establish a current free listing.

Proposed initial budget: **200 basic search calls/month**, independent of the number of pages refreshed. Weekly maintenance usually revisits known sources, not a fresh search per page. Avoid agent loops, expensive deep research and searches on every visitor request.

**Capacity estimate:** 300/week is 1,300/month; 1,000/week is 4,333/month using 52/12 weeks. If GET + cleanup averages 2 seconds/page and 10% need 10 additional browser seconds, workload is roughly 65/217 runner minutes/month before startup, retries and robots delays. At 3,000/week it is 650 minutes. These are **unmeasured assumptions**, not performance claims. A host requiring 30-second spacing can instead dominate the run: 4,333 × 30/60 ≈ 2,167 minutes if serialized. Spread work across allowed hosts and days, cache aggressively, or lower frequency; do not parallelize requests to defeat a host's delay. Include the project's ordinary CI consumption in private-runner capacity.

At a 10% rendering rate, Firecrawl's free pool covers the estimated 130/433/1,300 fallback pages/month at the first two volumes but not the third. Use bounded Playwright on Actions for permitted renderable pages if needed; leave unsupported pages for review. All public-page fetching remains GET-only. A browser may issue POSTs or analytics automatically: if used later under a GET-only policy, explicitly block non-GET subrequests and recognize that some pages will not work. No rendering was performed during this research.

## 3. Extraction models and unit economics

Convert HTML to markdown/main text locally first, but keep a separate loss-minimized source-text representation for quote verification. Trim using martial arts/cost/schedule sections and their surrounding context; preserve fee disclaimers, registration rules, season headings and age restrictions. Never just take the first tokens. Pages with conflicting sections or too much relevant text go to review/chunking within a fixed budget.

### Batch, caching and schema reliability

**VERIFIED:** Anthropic [Batch docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/batch-processing) give 50% savings, up to 24-hour processing/expiration, and warn batches may slightly exceed workspace spend limits. Submit small bounded batches, store batch IDs and retrieve results on a later scheduled run; do not hold a serverless request open. One expired/failed item must not invalidate successfully verified independent items or erase existing listings.

**VERIFIED:** Haiku's [pricing/caching documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/haiku-5-5/overview) lists 5-minute write $0.125/M, 1-hour write $0.20/M, read $0.01/M for the short-prompt tier; minimum cacheable prefix 512 tokens. Sonnet's [rates](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/sonnet-5-5/overview) are $2.50/$4/$0.10 respectively. Cache the stable schema/instructions across closely spaced calls only when economical. Variable page text is not a reusable prefix; repeated pages should bypass the model entirely. Do not inflate a short prompt merely to qualify for caching. Baseline estimates assume **no cache discount**.

**VERIFIED:** Claude's [structured-output docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs) use `output_config.format` with `type: "json_schema"`, with a supported-schema subset. Regardless of provider, decode through Effect Schema and handle refusals, truncation, unsupported schemas and API errors. A constrained schema limits output shape, not hallucination. No model was benchmarked here: first evaluate a volunteer-labeled set containing trials, required donations, equipment fees, registration-only offers, stale seasons, contradictory text and unrelated “free” words.

## 4. Verification design: evidence must survive independent checks

These are **PROPOSED** rules, not existing project behavior. The prototype currently has no review queue or staleness automation; any future queue must be private and must not imply the current database has approval states.

| Guardrail | Cheap implementation | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity/currentness | Establish source URL, NYC location, program identity, applicable ages/eligibility and season before price classification. Unknown fields remain null | General organization free policy needs a demonstrable link to this offering; archive/search/LLM answer alone cannot publish |
| Literal quote | Keep decoded, non-rewritten DOM text blocks and original response hash; require `block.includes(quote)` and record offsets/block ID | Missing substring always fails. No fuzzy matching, case folding, ellipsis insertion, whitespace repair or model-written paraphrase to make it pass |
| Canonical representation | Decode HTML entities and preserve text before markdown rewriting; document the versioned DOM-to-text policy. Keep mapping to captured DOM block | “Literal” means an exact span of fetched page text, not raw markup bytes (`&amp;` differs from `&`). Also check against source blocks, not only Jina/Readability output. If markdown changed a span, select a new exact source span |
| Visible/applicable text | Exclude script/style/comments/hidden templates; include relevant surrounding fee and eligibility blocks | A quote in a prompt injection, metadata spam, canceled class or unrelated program is not evidence |
| Cost lexicon | Detect `free`, `no charge`, `$0`, `complimentary`; also `donation`, `sliding scale`, `registration required`, `trial`, `intro`, `membership`, `equipment`, `deposit`, `fee`, `not free` | Keywords find conflicts, not truth. “Free parking”, “free consultation”, free for members, and a required donation cannot establish an unconditional free class |
| Qualification rules | Registration with explicit free evidence → `free_with_registration`; limited introductory session → `free_trial_only`; variable/required payment → `sliding_scale` when wording supports it | Missing/contradictory costs → `unverified`. Do not classify a price range as allowing $0 without explicit text. Optional donation wording needs clear no-payment-required context |
| Second model on disagreement | Only when deterministic rules and first model disagree, or trial/fees applicability remains ambiguous. Give both models the same bounded source blocks | Proposed ceiling 5% of extracted pages; over ceiling queue the rest. Judge cannot supply a missing quote or overrule an exact-match failure. Remaining conflict → human review |
| Confidence | Track separate evidence-match, applicability, temporal and completeness checks. Proposed model score threshold 0.95 only as triage | Self-reported confidence is uncalibrated. No threshold overrides a failed hard rule. Calibrate on labeled examples before automatic activation |
| Private human review | Show source link, exact quote highlighted in surrounding text, conflicts, changes, capture date and one proposed status | Record reviewer decision/provenance. Keep unresolved candidates hidden/unverified; do not change published records merely because a job was partial |
| Change detection | Conditional GET; separate byte hash, relevant-text hash and extractor/prompt/schema version. Sitemaps' lastmod is only a hint | 304/unchanged text avoids model cost but advances `last_seen_at`, not automatic cost verification. Template-only changes need not trigger extraction; changed fee/season blocks always do |
| Result cache | Key by source URL, relevant hash, extraction version and model ID; dedupe URLs and identical content | Do not reuse evidence for a different class just because text hashes match. Cache failures briefly; never cache a bot challenge as valid content |
| Dead links / freshness | Use GET rather than HEAD under this task's constraint; distinguish 404/410 from transient 429/5xx/403. Proposed weekly link check, 30-day cost review, earlier than event/season end | Immediate hidden/stale treatment for confirmed cancellation/ended season; proposed two separated failures for transient availability. Source failure alone does not delete history. Policy needs implementation |

**Evidence-capture nuance:** An unchanged page is not a new verification merely because its cache validator matches. Store latest successful check separately from `last_verified_at`. A deliberate new cost review can capture fresh evidence with the actual review time and method; do not recycle an old `captured_at` or generate artificial evidence rows to keep cards fresh. The RPC only advances verification for newly inserted matching evidence; adapters remain responsible for truth/applicability.

A substring check is necessary but insufficient: “This is **not** free” contains “free”. Validate the whole claim and nearby qualifications, and do not allow a second model to replace the source as authority.

### Extraction prompt / schema outline

Input envelope is server-supplied: `{source_url, final_url, fetched_at, response_hash, text_version, blocks:[{id,text}]}`. Treat page text as untrusted data, not instructions. The model has no browser, tools, SQL, secrets or authority to publish. Its task is to extract supported facts, preserve unknowns and abstain. Return one object per identifiable offering; never merge an entire organization into a class.

```text
Envelope: schema_version, offerings[], warnings[]
Offering:
  title: string | null
  organizer: string | null
  source_url: string                 # must be from supplied envelope
  source_identity: string | null     # source program/event ID, if present
  discipline_terms: string[]
  location: {venue, street_address, borough, neighborhood}: nullable strings
  ages: {min: integer|null, max: integer|null, source_text: string|null}
  schedule_text: string | null
  occurrences: [{starts_at, ends_at|null, timezone, source_occurrence_key|null}]
  season_text: string | null
  eligibility_text: string | null
  registration: {required: boolean|null, instructions: string|null, url: string|null}
  cost_status: free | free_with_registration | free_trial_only | sliding_scale | unverified
  cost_evidence: [{quote: string, block_id: string, supports_cost_status: enum}]
  qualification_evidence: [{quote: string, block_id: string, kind: string}]
  conflicts: string[]
  unknown_fields: string[]
  confidence: number                 # triage only
```

Use a closed schema (`additionalProperties: false`), bounded arrays/strings and explicit nullable fields. Require evidence for every non-unverified cost claim; quotes must be copied **verbatim and contiguously** from supplied original text blocks with enough context to support the exact status. Do not allow paraphrases or inserted ellipses. Missing evidence forces `unverified`; empty evidence is allowed only there. Server enforces cross-field conditions even if the provider's JSON Schema subset cannot. Preserve original schedule text; missing year/timezone/date never licenses invented occurrences. Normalize known NYC wall times server-side in `America/New_York`; validate DST ambiguities.

The server supplies capture timestamps, source IDs, `discovered_by`, final status, dedupe key and verification method; reject model attempts to invent them. Model output is an intermediate format, not the RPC payload. Resolve taxonomy and source identity deterministically; manual cross-source merges only.

## 5. Lowest-cost end-to-end pipeline and monthly budget

1. **Discover weekly on GitHub Actions.** Start from existing curated registry/deterministic seeds, approved organization links and opt-in submissions. Spend at most the proposed 200 basic Tavily searches/month. Store leads, never publish search snippets. Select one provider and stop at its free allowance; no automatic provider hopping on quota exhaustion.
2. **Fetch on Actions.** Robots/terms allowlist first, per-host pacing, conditional GET, bounded retries and conservative redirect handling. Clean HTML locally, read JSON-LD opportunistically, calculate relevant-content hashes. Render only a capped subset of permitted pages with Playwright; stop on protection. Prefer no hosted reader/browser dependency initially.
3. **Extract only changed, relevant pages.** One `claude-haiku-5-5` structured-output request, fixed input/output budgets. Default synchronous calls are operationally simpler at this scale. Add Batch only if savings justify managing submission/result retrieval; retain previous verified state while waiting. Absolute $0 mode can queue work for Gemini/Workers AI's free allowance instead.
4. **Verify on Actions using shared server-domain logic.** Effect Schema decode, exact quote/applicability checks, eligibility and temporal checks; optional standard-rate Luna judge for at most the proposed 5% disputed subset. Unresolved cases become private candidates; validated offerings may become active without a blanket approval gate, matching the spec.
5. **Store through the existing ingestion boundary.** Future Actions integration can call an authenticated bounded Vercel ingestion endpoint that validates candidates itself and invokes `ingest_listing` atomically; Actions must not be trusted to send an unchecked `active` flag. Normalized snapshots, occurrences and append-only evidence go to Supabase, with stable `(source_id, dedupe_key)` and source-run serialization. No service-role key in public JSON, client bundles or logs. This future private write is an implementation proposal; only public GET research was performed here.
6. **Vercel serves the directory; daily Cron does small housekeeping.** Cron checks freshness/run health and handles short trusted-source jobs within duration limits. Actions runs its own schedule; no long scrape inside a Cron request. Supabase remains durable source/listing/evidence/run state; a new cache/review schema would require a separate future change. Do not assume those tables already exist.

### Hard spend cap, not just alerts

**PROPOSED design:** reserve **$15/month maximum for all metered services combined**, leaving roughly $5 for taxes/unexpected fixed costs within the target. Keep paid search/PAYG browsers off. Before each paid call atomically reserve its worst-case input + maximum billed output/tool fee in a Postgres monthly ledger; allow it only if `spent + outstanding_reservations + next_reservation <= cap`. A single monthly owner/key scope and source-run lock prevent concurrent workers racing the cap.

Commit measured usage after response; on timeout/unknown billing retain the reservation until reconciliation. Each retry requires a new reservation. Include all in-flight batch items before submission; use bounded batches because provider enforcement can lag. If token counting is unavailable, use a conservative bound or reject the request. Enforce total output including reasoning, input bytes/tokens, calls/page, page/domain/run counts, redirect depth and retry counts. Stop below the cap if actual pricing/model differs from the approved rate card. No recursive agent/tool loops or fallback to pricier models.

Vendor spend controls/prepaid balances are a second backstop, not proof of an absolute cap: delayed charges, outstanding jobs and tax may exceed a dashboard limit. Keep auto-recharge disabled, alert at proposed $5/$10/$13, and queue remaining work at the ceiling. These are future design choices, not settings changed by this research. No accounts or paid capacity were created.

## 8. Risks and decisions before implementation

- **Legal/ToS/robots:** robots permission and copyright/reuse permission are separate. Record allowlisted host, applicable user-agent policy, crawl delay, terms/license, decision date and opt-out contact. Honor the source's restrictions even through a reader/crawler vendor; 403/429/challenges stop that path. Public GET access alone is not a grant to redistribute descriptions. Keep short necessary evidence excerpts, link the source, and seek permission where terms are unclear. No legal conclusion of blanket scraping permission is made here.
- **Hosting eligibility:** the $0 Vercel assumption depends on Hobby's personal/non-commercial terms, not just nonprofit status. Check approved hosting before committing to production economics; free-plan availability/quotas can change. This is a material open dependency in every monthly total.
- **Privacy:** the directory concerns youth programs, not individual children. Never collect attendee names, school/health/referral details or private contact lists. Separate public facts/evidence from private organizer contacts and review notes; use RLS and short retention. Free inference data-use policies are not interchangeable.
- **Hallucinated free access:** exact-match alone does not prove applicability, absence of required fees, registration conditions or currentness. Fail closed on missing/conflicting evidence; trials/sliding scale stay distinct and opt-in filters match the spec. Seasonal 2026 programs in existing research must not roll forward to winter.
- **Prompt injection / malicious URLs:** treat fetched text and model output as untrusted. No model tools or secrets. Validate redirects and DNS/IP destinations, reject private/link-local networks and credentials in URLs, limit response sizes and recursion; sanitize any displayed HTML. Never fetch arbitrary model-invented links without the same source-access checks.
- **Runaway spend:** fixed search quota, no automatic recharges, no paid browser default, atomic reservations including retries/in-flight batches, capped model output and a kill switch. Alerts alone are not a hard cap. Source changes and adversarial giant pages must reduce coverage, not expand spend.
- **Vendor lock-in/model lifecycle:** keep normalized input blocks, versioned JSON schema, evidence-validation logic and provider interface independent. Pin model/price configurations; re-evaluate announced migrations. Do not cache generated facts without prompt/model version and source hash. Export normalized records without proprietary search-result payloads.
- **Volunteer maintenance:** begin with the curated high-yield hosts, one search provider and one extraction provider. No vector DB, always-on browser service or local GPU dependency. Measure changed fraction, parse rejection rate, evidence failures, judge fraction, review age and dollars before increasing coverage. A persistent large queue means narrow scope or recruit reviewers.
- **Freshness and partial failure:** success counts and rejected candidates belong in private ingestion runs. Only a complete bounded snapshot can reconcile absence; a failed fetch is not cancellation. Keep evidence history and stable source keys; explicit cancellation/season end needs a deliberate state transition. Staleness/review automation remains future work.
